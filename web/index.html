READ BEFORE YOU RUN
RUNTIME / ANDROID

Your phone is the host.

Allow Termux to run in the background and disable its battery optimization. Android can still suspend it. Force-stopping Termux or restarting your phone stops the bot; use the shortcut to start it again.

SESSION / LOCAL

Your session stays local.

Your saved WhatsApp session lives in Termux, not your browser. Clearing browser data won’t erase it, but uninstalling Termux or clearing its app data will. Reopen the widget shortcut if your dashboard locks.

HOSTING / SEPARATE

The site is not the bot.

This static page can live on Vercel. The bot runs on your Android, not on Vercel. Hosted pages never contact the local API. Your login and bot controls stay on the local dashboard.
